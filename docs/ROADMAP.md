# Project roadmap

The project is split into three major phases. Details of each phase are in its own plan document.

| Phase | Contents | Status | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Materials** | Reference registry, curriculum, pedagogy design, content production | In progress | [PLAN-PHASE-1.md](PLAN-PHASE-1.md) |
| **2. Simulation system** | Simulation engine based on queueing theory, components, failure scenarios, lab grading | Not started | written when Phase 1 reaches M3 |
| **3. Application** | Web app: lessons, quizzes, lab canvas, progress, spaced repetition, beta & launch | Not started | written when Phase 2 reaches a prototype |

The phases are **not strictly sequential**. After pedagogy design is done (M3), content production (M4) continues while the simulator and app are built. Lab specs written in Phase 1 become input for Phase 2.

## Principles for all phases

1. Every claim has a source from the Reference Registry.
2. Rewrite, don't copy. Concepts may be used; original sentences and diagrams may not.
3. Practice over memorization. Every module ends with something designed or fixed.
4. Simulation must be honest. Models rest on queueing theory and documented behavior.
5. Free and sustainable. Cost per user is close to zero.
6. Reviewed before publishing. AI helps with drafting; verification is still mandatory.
7. Questions do not leak the answer.

## Short positioning

A mix of the daily-habit approach of Devmaxx (lessons, quizzes, streaks, spaced repetition) with PRISM-style architecture simulation (canvas, traffic, failure scenarios), fully free, in Indonesian, backend-focused. The full comparison research is in the master plan document (Claude Docs).
